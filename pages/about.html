The Story

Built by a creator, for creators

Charlotte Graham

The vision behind the space

Space shapes content. Bad spaces produce mediocre work regardless of how much skill walks through the door.

Charlotte Graham spent years shooting on location across Southeast Asia, watching talented creatives battle with substandard spaces, broken equipment, and zero creative support. Studio by Char is the answer to that problem.

Every set was designed the way Charlotte wanted to shoot. Every piece of equipment was chosen the way she wanted to work. The result is a studio that feels less like a rental and more like a collaborator.
